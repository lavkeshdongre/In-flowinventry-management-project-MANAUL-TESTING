InFlow Inventory Management System – Manual Testing
1. Project Overview
InFlow Inventory Management System is an inventory management application designed to help businesses manage and track their inventory operations efficiently.

The application allows users to manage products, suppliers, customers, purchases, sales, and stock levels. The main objective of this project is to verify that all inventory-related functionalities work correctly and meet the expected requirements.
2. Project Objective
The objective of this project is to perform manual testing to identify defects, validate application functionality, and ensure the system works as expected.
3. Testing Scope
The following modules are covered in this project:
- Product Management: Verify product creation, editing, deletion, and product details.
- Purchase Management: Verify purchase orders, item selection, quantity, and unit price.
- Sales Management: Verify sales transactions and product availability.
- Supplier Management: Verify supplier registration and information updates.
- Customer Management: Verify customer details and management.
- Inventory Management: Verify stock availability and quantity updates.
